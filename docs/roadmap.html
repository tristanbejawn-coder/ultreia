October onwardAfter the walk

Ultreia for other walkers

The house exists; this is the front door. Nothing here changes what Ju and Jit use.

  • Sign-in by email magic link; a walk belongs to an owner.
  • Route picker on the catalogue: choose a Camino, its variants, adjust stages, name the walkers, upload the photo.
  • One-off payment per walk through Stripe Checkout; following stays free and account-free.Stripe account, terms & privacy page
  • Paid map tiles (MapTiler or Mapbox) once it’s a product; the free imagery is fine for one family.
  • The printed book of Ju and Jit’s walk, laid out from the same data.

What could bite, and the answer

Photo location on iPhoneiOS sometimes hands the browser a converted copy without GPS. Rehearsal Sunday tests it; the phone’s own location is the built-in fallback and the day’s check-in the fallback behind that.
Signal in GaliciaPosts queue on the phone and drain when a bar appears; the public page keeps its last state offline. Nothing they do needs a connection at the moment they do it.
The Caminha boatIt runs on tide and weather. That’s exactly why the fork is chosen on the day, from their screen, not planned.
Video before the cap worksClips don’t ship until Cloudflare enforces the 30 seconds server-side, so there is no window where a long video can break playback.
Imagery licensingFree Esri tiles with attribution are fine for a family page. The moment other walkers pay, tiles move to a keyed provider on a paid plan.
Me, mid-walkEvery drop after the 9th is additive and deploys from main; if something goes wrong the previous deploy is one click back in Netlify.
