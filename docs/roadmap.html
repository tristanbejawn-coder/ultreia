Sat 5 – Sun 13 SepRehearsal, on their phones

Proven where it matters

  • The camera roll opens natively on iPhone: the button is a real file input now, not a scripted click Safari ignores.
  • A photograph placed by hand lands there, not at 0,0 in the Atlantic. A picture with no place can go up anyway, under Not on the road.
  • A walker’s link opens the map, with a floating dock underneath: photo, clip, diary, where we are, arrived, the post. Their links open and copy from your account.
  • Clips and diaries go straight to storage by signed link: 30 seconds, 48 MB, a poster frame, and a diary recorded from the front camera. This replaced the Cloudflare plan, so there’s no account to set up.
  • Bad signal can’t double a photo. Each queued post carries its own id; a post the server refuses tells the walker instead of vanishing.
  • Background trackers of any shape: OwnTracks, GPSLogger or Traccar, same throttle and off-route rules.
  • Push, both ways. Followers get a bell when a stage completes; the walkers get one when the post is in at 19:00, fired by a scheduled function. One phone subscribed so far.
  • The whole route is real. Stage one re-traced down the Douro to Foz and up the boardwalks (35 km on the ground); every stage to Santiago drawn from OpenStreetMap, both boat crossings excepted.
  • A scrapbook of their own. Any picture can be kept for the two of them: it shows blue on their map, never reaches the family’s page, and is served by an expiring link.
  • The demo walk is gone from code, repository and database. One walk, theirs, starting Monday.

October onwardAfter the walk

Ultreia for other walkers

The front door is mostly built already. What’s left is proving it with someone who isn’t family.

  • Sign-in, owner accounts, the route picker and Checkout are in the code.
  • Switched on for strangers: live Stripe key, the webhook registered from /account (not done yet), a terms page read through.you
  • Paid map tiles (MapTiler or Mapbox) once it’s a product; free imagery is fine for one family.
  • The printed book of Ju and Jit’s walk, laid out from the same data, scrapbook pictures included if they want them.

What could bite, and the answer

Photo location on iPhoneTested on the day: Ju’s first photograph landed at the cathedral. The phone’s own location and a hand-placed town are there if a later one comes through without GPS.
Signal in GaliciaPosts queue and drain when a bar appears, and a retry can no longer double one. The family’s page keeps its last state offline.
The Caminha boatIt runs on tide and weather, which is why the fork is chosen on Thursday evening, on their screen, not planned.
A clip Safari can’t readIt goes up anyway without a poster frame; one over 48 MB is refused with a message saying what to do. Nothing is lost silently.
Two sessions in one repoTwice already two builders changed the same checkout at once: a duplicate demo walk, and a branch switched mid-task. During the walk, one session at a time, and every change through a pull request.
Test data on a live walkA private test post is on their walk right now. From tomorrow, rehearse on a separate walk, never on theirs.
Imagery licensingFree Esri tiles with attribution are fine for a family page. The moment other walkers pay, tiles move to a keyed provider on a paid plan.
Me, mid-walkEvery change deploys from main and is additive; if one goes wrong, the previous deploy is one click back in Netlify.
